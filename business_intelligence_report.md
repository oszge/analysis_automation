## Key Insights

- Fashion was the strongest category overall and monthly, generating EUR 21966.06, up EUR 732.83 (+3.45%). Home & Living also grew by EUR 360.32 (+3.53%).
- Toys & Games was the largest category drag, declining by EUR 3508.55 (-19.79%) to EUR 14219.8.
- The United Kingdom remained the leading monthly country at EUR 20296.07 and grew by EUR 1815.4 (+9.82%). Spain delivered the fastest country growth, up EUR 2296.67 (+24.65%) to EUR 11612.33.
- Netherlands and Belgium were the largest country declines, falling by EUR 3560.41 (-34.61%) and EUR 2557.66 (-36.21%) respectively. Italy also declined by EUR 1769.49 (-14.64%).
- Canvas Sneakers was the best product overall and increased by EUR 2157.29 (+62.92%) to EUR 5585.9. Action Camera rose by EUR 1611.37 (+84.35%), but this percentage was supported by a relatively low previous revenue base of EUR 1910.23 and should be interpreted cautiously.
- The largest product declines were Bluetooth Speaker, down EUR 2452.98 (-35.39%), and Building Blocks Set, down EUR 2349.75 (-39.2%).
- Monthly transactions increased from 960 to 980, while units declined from 1605 to 1593. This indicates weaker unit volume despite more transactions and may signal changes in product mix or basket size.
- Weekly units increased from 355 to 397 and weekly transactions increased from 219 to 244, alongside the EUR 2044.9 revenue increase.

## Risks

- Monthly revenue is declining despite transactions increasing from 960 to 980, suggesting that transaction growth is not translating into sufficient revenue improvement.
- The concentration of declines in Toys & Games, Bluetooth Speaker, Building Blocks Set, Netherlands, and Belgium creates material exposure to specific product and market weaknesses.
- The strong growth percentages for products such as Action Camera may overstate underlying momentum because of relatively low previous-period revenue bases.
- Winter Boots, Board Game, Wool Scarf, Puzzle 1000 Pieces, and Power Bank also declined, increasing the risk that weakness extends beyond a single category or product.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize a recovery plan for Toys & Games, focusing on Board Game, Building Blocks Set, and Puzzle 1000 Pieces, which all recorded monthly declines.
- Investigate the causes of revenue deterioration in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Italy, including availability, pricing, promotion, and channel performance before increasing investment in those markets.
- Protect and expand momentum in Fashion, especially Canvas Sneakers, while maintaining focus on the United Kingdom and Spain, the strongest reported growth markets.
- Review Bluetooth Speaker performance urgently, as it recorded the largest product revenue decline, and assess whether inventory, pricing, or promotional changes are required.
- Use the weekly improvement as a basis for targeted campaigns, but monitor whether the recovery persists before treating it as a sustained trend.
- Track revenue alongside units and transactions to identify whether lower revenue is being driven by product mix, pricing, or weaker unit volume.
