## Key Insights

- Weekly growth was supported by higher volume: units increased from 355 to 397 and transactions from 219 to 244.
- Fashion remained the strongest category, generating 21966.06 EUR monthly and increasing by 732.83 EUR or 3.45%. It also led weekly revenue at 6303.31 EUR.
- The largest monthly category decline was Toys & Games, down 3508.55 EUR or 19.79%, making it the primary category drag.
- Spain was the strongest country by growth, up 2296.67 EUR or 24.65%. The United Kingdom remained the largest country, with 20296.07 EUR and growth of 1815.4 EUR or 9.82%.
- The largest country declines were the Netherlands, down 3560.41 EUR or 34.61%, and Belgium, down 2557.66 EUR or 36.21%. Italy also declined by 1769.49 EUR or 14.64%.
- Action Camera grew by 1611.37 EUR or 84.35%, and Canvas Sneakers grew by 2157.29 EUR or 62.92%. These unusually large percentages should be interpreted alongside their relatively low previous revenues of 1910.23 EUR and 3428.61 EUR.
- Bluetooth Speaker and Building Blocks Set were the largest product-level declines, down 2452.98 EUR or 35.39% and 2349.75 EUR or 39.2%, respectively. Winter Boots also fell by 1104.06 EUR or 14.91%.
- Monthly transactions increased from 960 to 980 while units declined from 1605 to 1593, indicating that the monthly revenue decline was not caused by fewer transactions alone.

## Risks

- The monthly revenue decline of 3121.2 EUR, or 4.22%, indicates that weekly recovery has not yet translated into sustained monthly growth.
- Toys & Games represents a significant category risk because its decline of 3508.55 EUR exceeds the declines reported for other categories.
- The Netherlands and Belgium showed particularly sharp country declines of 34.61% and 36.21%, creating concentration and regional demand risks.
- Several major products declined materially, especially Bluetooth Speaker, Building Blocks Set, Board Game, and Power Bank.
- Growth in Action Camera and Canvas Sneakers may appear disproportionate because the previous-period revenue bases were comparatively small; continuation should be validated over additional periods.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize recovery actions for Toys & Games, focusing on Board Game, Building Blocks Set, Plush Animal, and Puzzle 1000 Pieces performance.
- Investigate country-specific causes of decline in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Italy, including availability, pricing, promotion, and fulfilment issues.
- Protect and scale proven growth in Fashion, Canvas Sneakers, and the United Kingdom while monitoring whether demand can be sustained beyond the current period.
- Review inventory, merchandising, and promotional support for Bluetooth Speaker, Building Blocks Set, and Winter Boots.
- Use the weekly improvement as a basis for targeted campaigns, but assess performance using both revenue and volume metrics before increasing investment.
- Validate the strong percentage growth of Action Camera and Canvas Sneakers against additional periods to distinguish sustained demand from a low-base effect.
