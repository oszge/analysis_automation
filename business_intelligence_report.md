## Key Insights

- Fashion was the leading category with monthly revenue of EUR 21,966.06 and grew by EUR 732.83 or 3.45%. Home & Living also increased by EUR 360.32 or 3.53%.
- Toys & Games was the largest category drag, declining by EUR 3,508.55 or 19.79%. Consumer Tech declined by EUR 568.12 or 3.29%, while Wellness decreased by EUR 137.68 or 1.84%.
- Spain delivered the strongest country growth, increasing by EUR 2,296.67 or 24.65%. The United Kingdom, the best country, grew by EUR 1,815.40 or 9.82% to EUR 20,296.07.
- The Netherlands, Belgium, and Italy were significant country-level declines, down EUR 3,560.41 or 34.61%, EUR 2,557.66 or 36.21%, and EUR 1,769.49 or 14.64%, respectively.
- Action Camera grew by 84.35% or EUR 1,611.37, while Canvas Sneakers grew by 62.92% or EUR 2,157.29 and remained the best product overall. The unusually high Action Camera percentage is partly explained by its relatively small previous revenue baseline of EUR 1,910.23.
- The largest product declines were Bluetooth Speaker, down EUR 2,452.98 or 35.39%; Building Blocks Set, down EUR 2,349.75 or 39.20%; Winter Boots, down EUR 1,104.06 or 14.91%; and Board Game, down EUR 1,009.70 or 16.63%.
- Monthly units decreased from 1,605 to 1,593, while transactions increased from 960 to 980. This indicates weaker unit volume despite more transactions, although no average transaction value is supplied.
- Weekly units increased from 355 to 397 and weekly transactions increased from 219 to 244, supporting the weekly revenue recovery.

## Risks

- The monthly revenue decline of EUR 3,121.20 or 4.22% persists despite the positive weekly trend, creating a risk that the latest improvement is temporary.
- Revenue is exposed to severe weakness in the Netherlands and Belgium, where declines were 34.61% and 36.21%, respectively.
- Toys & Games shows broad pressure, with category revenue down EUR 3,508.55 or 19.79%; several related products also declined materially, including Building Blocks Set, Board Game, and Puzzle 1000 Pieces.
- The decline in key products such as Bluetooth Speaker, Building Blocks Set, and Winter Boots may indicate demand, availability, or seasonal execution issues, but the supplied data does not identify the underlying cause.
- Growth in Action Camera should be monitored carefully because its 84.35% increase is based on previous revenue of only EUR 1,910.23 and may not represent a sustained trend.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize recovery actions for Toys & Games, especially Building Blocks Set, Board Game, and Puzzle 1000 Pieces, while reviewing availability, pricing, promotion, and demand by market.
- Investigate the Netherlands, Belgium, and Italy declines before increasing investment there; check market-specific assortment, delivery performance, pricing, and campaign effectiveness.
- Scale proven growth areas, particularly Fashion, Canvas Sneakers, Action Camera, Spain, and the United Kingdom, while validating whether recent product growth is repeatable.
- Use the positive weekly movement in units, transactions, and revenue to test targeted campaigns, rather than assuming the monthly decline has been resolved.
- Protect Fashion leadership and support Home & Living growth, while addressing the smaller declines in Consumer Tech and Wellness through focused product-level actions.
- Track weekly performance against the monthly baseline and separately monitor products with large percentage changes and small previous-period revenue to avoid overinterpreting percentage growth.
