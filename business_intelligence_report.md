## Key Insights

- Fashion was the best category overall and monthly, generating EUR 21966.06 in the month versus EUR 21233.23 previously, up EUR 732.83 or 3.45%.
- The United Kingdom was the best country, with monthly revenue of EUR 20296.07 versus EUR 18480.67 previously, up EUR 1815.4 or 9.82%. It also led weekly revenue at EUR 5073.42.
- Toys & Games was the largest category drag, falling to EUR 14219.8 from EUR 17728.35, down EUR 3508.55 or 19.79%. Consumer Tech also declined by EUR 568.12 or 3.29%.
- Spain was the strongest country by percentage and absolute growth, increasing to EUR 11612.33 from EUR 9315.66, up EUR 2296.67 or 24.65%. Ireland and France also grew by EUR 270.68 and EUR 383.61 respectively.
- The Netherlands and Belgium were major geographic risks, declining by EUR 3560.41 or 34.61% and EUR 2557.66 or 36.21% respectively. Italy also fell by EUR 1769.49 or 14.64%.
- Action Camera and Canvas Sneakers recorded strong growth of EUR 1611.37 or 84.35% and EUR 2157.29 or 62.92% respectively. These percentages should be interpreted with caution because their previous revenues were relatively small at EUR 1910.23 and EUR 3428.61.
- The largest product declines were Bluetooth Speaker, down EUR 2452.98 or 35.39%, Building Blocks Set, down EUR 2349.75 or 39.2%, and Winter Boots, down EUR 1104.06 or 14.91%.
- Weekly units increased from 355 to 397 and transactions increased from 219 to 244. Monthly units declined from 1605 to 1593, while transactions increased from 960 to 980, indicating weaker revenue despite more monthly transactions.

## Risks

- The monthly revenue decline of EUR 3121.2 or 4.22% indicates that the positive weekly trend has not yet translated into sustained monthly growth.
- Toys & Games represents the largest category decline, while Building Blocks Set, Board Game, and Puzzle 1000 Pieces all decreased, suggesting broad weakness within the category.
- Revenue weakness is concentrated in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Italy, creating a material regional performance risk.
- Bluetooth Speaker and Building Blocks Set experienced the largest product-level revenue losses, which may indicate demand, availability, pricing, or promotional issues.
- Strong growth percentages for Action Camera and Canvas Sneakers are based on lower previous-period revenues, so the percentage gains may overstate their underlying scale.

## Recommendations

- Prioritize recovery actions for Toys & Games, focusing on Building Blocks Set, Board Game, and Puzzle 1000 Pieces through targeted merchandising, promotion, and availability checks.
- Investigate the causes of decline in the Netherlands, Belgium, and Italy, including local demand, fulfillment, pricing, and campaign performance before increasing investment in those markets.
- Protect and expand momentum in Fashion, Canvas Sneakers, Spain, and the United Kingdom, which are the clearest current growth contributors.
- Review inventory, pricing, and promotional execution for Bluetooth Speaker, Winter Boots, and other products with large absolute revenue declines.
- Use weekly performance as an early signal, but monitor the next monthly result to confirm whether the increase from EUR 15541.32 to EUR 17586.22 represents a sustained recovery.
- Assess revenue per transaction and product mix because monthly transactions rose from 960 to 980 while revenue fell from EUR 73890.09 to EUR 70768.89.
